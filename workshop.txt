description=
description=Negatives
description=- Selective Eater: Eating processed food without preparing it increases Unhappiness. Cold or raw food or eating directly from the pot also increases a bit of unhapiness each.
description=- Vocal in Pain: Tends to cry out whenever a new injury is suffered, even a minor one.
description=- Fumbles Under pressure: Exertion while panicked may loosen bandages or reopen stitches. The chance increases with Panic.
description=- Thrill Seeker: Going more than two days without reaching at least 50% Stress or Panic causes severe Boredom.
description=- Must Finish the Job: Cancelling an action before it is completed may cause Stress.
description=
description=Positives
description=- Neck Reflex: While not out of breath or exhausted, neck injuries are turned into scratches. Loses a lot of endurance when activated.
description=- Meditate: While sitting, panic and stress are gradually reduced.
description=- Road Tripper: Driving a moving vehicle slowly reduces Stress and Unhappiness.
description=- High Spirits: Recovers Endurance faster if not unhappy in the slightest.
description=- Bitten? No.: The first time you get bitten, the injury gets turned in a laceration, also saving you from the Knox Infection.
